Process

How Niche Edits Work

1

We Find Relevant Existing Content

Our team searches for aged, already-ranking articles in your niche using Ahrefs. We look for pages with organic traffic and real authority — not new posts that haven't been indexed yet.

2

We Contact the Publisher

We reach out to the site owner or editor and negotiate a contextual link insertion in the most relevant paragraph of the article. We pitch value — not just a transaction.

3

Link Goes Live & You Get Your Report

Your link is inserted contextually, the page re-indexes, and you receive a full report: live URL, DR, organic traffic, anchor text, and indexed status. Average live time: 7–14 days.
