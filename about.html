Meet the Team
Our Story

Founded by SEOs who were tired of grey-hat shortcuts

We spent years running in-house SEO for SaaS and e-commerce brands, watching rankings evaporate every time a "cheap" link package got caught in a core update. So we started vetting sites the hard way — real traffic, real editors, real relevance — and turned that process into a service.

Today our team works directly with agencies and in-house marketers who need a link partner they can hand target pages to and trust the placements that come back, every single time.
