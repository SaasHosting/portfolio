Blogger Outreach, also known as influencer marketing or guest posting, is a link-building strategy that involves collaborating with relevant bloggers in your niche. By offering free content or partnerships, you create articles that align with the blog's audience while naturally incorporating a backlink to your website. Outreach Nomads' specialist team secures all mentions as editorial links from within the flow of an impartial, non-promotional blog post placement.
Outreach Nomads' blogger outreach service differs from traditional guest posting by offering more natural link placement. While guest posts often include links in an author bio, blogger outreach integrates editorial links directly within the content, written in collaboration with the blog owner. This type of link placement is seen as more authoritative by search engines like Google.
Absolutely! You choose the DR (Domain Rating) metrics you require for each placement. Domain Rating is updated regularly, so our system will show you the DR metrics at the time of placement. We use Ahrefs' Domain Rating (DR).
You don't need to worry about link history. Our dashboard, powered by the Ahrefs API, cross-references live Ahrefs link data to ensure we never duplicate link placements.
We have exceptionally talented in-house writers who will create original, magazine-quality content designed to educate the website owner's target audience while naturally incorporating your client's link. Unlike guest blogging services, the content will appear to be written by the blog owner, for a more natural result.
Absolutely! Our Blogger Outreach Services allow you to select any quantity during the ordering process and customize your Domain Rating requirements and word counts. Just input your anchor text and target URLs for each entry. You can tag orders in your dashboard to help track which clients you have ordered for within each order.
While you won't be able to pre-approve blog post placements, you can track the live progress and view secured placements directly in your dashboard. The content is non-promotional and written in a ghost-blogging style, making pre-approval unnecessary.
Our blogger outreach campaigns secure placements on English-speaking blogs. For UK, AUS & CAN clients, we always look for blogs from those countries in the first instance, but we cannot guarantee them 100% of the time. In these cases, we fall back on blogs that have a worldwide audience (.com/.net, etc), which still pass the link authority.
Blogger Outreach placements typically last indefinitely, and we offer a lifetime guarantee on all links. Most will remain active for many years. If a link is removed or placement goes down, let us know, and we'll be happy to replace it for you.
